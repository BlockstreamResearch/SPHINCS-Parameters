SHRINCS Parameter Explorer

Search SLH-DSA-compatible parameter sets as in the SHRINCS Parameter Search report.

Scheme

The stateless schemes of the report:

  • SPX: SLH-DSA, with WOTS and FORS.
  • W+C: WOTS+C instead of WOTS.
  • FORS+C: WOTS+C, and FORS+C, which grinds away the last FORS tree.
  • PORS+FP: WOTS+C, and PORS+FP, a single tree with Octopus authentication.

Winternitz parameter

The pool always includes w = 2, 4, 16 and 256, whose digits fit whole bytes as SLH-DSA requires. w = 32, 64 and 128 do not, so they are exploratory and extend only the SPX pool.

Bounds

Keep candidates within these multiples of SLH-DSA-128s.

Filter and rank on worst-case SigVer and ρ, against the worst case of SLH-DSA-128s ( C instead of C). WOTS+C verification costs the same for every message, so candidates with WOTS+C keep their values and gain headroom.

Weights

The best candidate has the smallest distance: the weighted root mean square of its five ratios to SLH-DSA-128s, each rescaled to 0–1 across the candidates within the bounds.

A higher weight gives a metric more say in the ranking.
