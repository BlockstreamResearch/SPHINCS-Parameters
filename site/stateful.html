SHRINCS Stateful Comparison

Compare stateful schemes with SLH-DSA-128s, or add your own with + Add candidate.

A cached signer keeps tree state between signatures: a BDS state for XMSS and XMSS-MT, and a leaf cache for UXMSS. A cold signer keeps none and rebuilds its trees for every signature. SLH-DSA-128s is stateless, so its SigGen is the same in both modes.

Sizes follow the SHRINCS stateful layout: a one-byte indicator, a 16-byte randomizer and the leaf index, then per layer the WOTS+C counter (2 bytes; 4 at w = 65536), the chains and the authentication path.

UXMSS signatures grow by one 16-byte node per signature, so size and ρ show first → last. Ratios, bounds, sorting and SigVer use the largest signature.

Cached: an upper bound per signature. For XMSS and XMSS-MT that is one BDS update per layer, at most (h′ − K)/2 + 1 leaf computations; for UXMSS it is the first signature, which combines the most nodes from the leaf cache. Cold: the trees are rebuilt for every signature.

What a cached signer stores: all hsf + 1 leaf hashes for UXMSS, the BDS state bound for XMSS and XMSS-MT. Empty for SLH-DSA-128s and for a cold signer.

All stateful candidates
